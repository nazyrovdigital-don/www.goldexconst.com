Questions

How much does it cost to hide TV wires?

In-wall wire hiding is an add-on to TV mounting (from $129). It's quoted before the visit based on your wall. Text a photo for a fast price.

Can you hide wires on any wall?

Most drywall walls work well. Brick, stone, tile and some exterior walls may not allow in-wall runs, so we'll suggest the cleanest option for your wall.

Can you mount a soundbar too?

Yes. Soundbar mounting is available as an add-on in the same visit.
