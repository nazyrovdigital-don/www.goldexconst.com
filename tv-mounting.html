Questions

Do I need to buy the wall mount?

If you already have one, we install it. If not, we can bring one that fits your TV — just tell us your TV size and we'll include it in your quote.

Can you hide the wires?

Yes. We can tidy cables on the wall or run them inside the wall for a clean look. In-wall concealment is an add-on, quoted before the visit.

What walls can you mount on?

Drywall over wood studs is the standard price. Brick, stone, tile and above-fireplace installs take extra hardware and time, so we quote those upfront.

How long does it take?

Most standard installs fit within the 2-hour appointment.

How do I pay?

Cash, check (payable to GOLDEX CONSTRUCTION LLC), Zelle or Venmo.
