Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GOLDEX Construction LLC ("GOLDEX," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit goldexconst.com or request an estimate from us. We provide interior construction and remodeling services in San Diego County, California.

Information we collect

When you submit the estimate request form, we collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The type of project you are interested in
  • Your city, ZIP code, or project address
  • Any project details you write in the message box

We also record the page you submitted the form from and, if you arrived through an advertisement or campaign link, the campaign details in that link (such as utm_source). If you send us photos or plans by text message or email, we receive those as well.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, review your project, and prepare an estimate
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your project
  • To schedule site visits and manage active jobs
  • To keep records of customer inquiries and improve our services
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed to do so. These include the tools we use to receive form submissions, store customer records and lead lists, send email, and exchange text messages. These providers may process your information on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or property.

Text messages and calls

By submitting the form or contacting us, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can reply STOP to any text message to opt out at any time, or tell us by phone or email. Opting out of marketing-type messages will not stop us from contacting you about an active estimate or job. We do not share mobile phone numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and analytics

Our website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser information when the page loads. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy.

How long we keep information

We keep customer and inquiry records for as long as needed to provide our services, meet legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you give us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to request to know what personal information we have collected about you, to request that we delete it or correct it, and not to be treated differently for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
